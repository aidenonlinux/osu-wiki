# Skin Editor
The **skin editor** is a feature in the *lazer* release stream that lets you customise the layout of your skin.

In there you can change:
- The layout of the HUD and other things
- **NOT** the looks of hitcircles or the hitounds

## How to access?
1. First of all, open your osu!(lazer) client.
2. Click **"Edit"** in the main menu.
3. Then click **"Skin Editor"**
